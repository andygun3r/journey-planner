Signaller
Clear direction for every journey. A confident, no-nonsense identity built around the UK rail network — and the people who signal you through it.
01 — Brand Story
Named for the person who tells trains where to go.
A signaller is the person at Network Rail who directs trains safely along the line — calm, precise, always in control. That's the promise behind the product: turn the chaos of UK transport into one clear signal.
We start with UK rail, built for daily commuters, leisure travellers and business travellers alike, with other transport modes joining over time. Where Trainline sells tickets and Citymapper maps the city, Signaller's job is singular: tell you clearly what's happening and what to do next.

02 — Logo
A banner repeater signal: a black arm raised across a white disc at the "off" angle. Direct, unambiguous, always in position.
Signaller
Signaller
Construction
The mark is drawn from a real UK rail banner repeater: a white disc, rimmed in navy, with a solid arm pivoting through its centre. At 45° it reads "off" — clear, proceed — which is the only position Signaller ever shows. The arm spans 130% of the disc's width and 13% of its height. Never redraw freehand — always scale the master file proportionally.
Clear space & minimum size
Clear space on all sides equals the height of the mark itself. Never let text, imagery or UI chrome cross that boundary.
Minimum digital size: 24px square. Minimum print size: 10mm square. Below that, drop the wordmark and use the icon alone.

06 — Iconography
Geometric, line-based, built on a strict grid — like signage pictograms, never decorative.
{{ ic.label }}
Stroke weight is consistent at 2px on a 24px grid, always navy or white, never red — red stays reserved for live alerts. Corners are either fully square or fully rounded within one icon set; never mixed.
